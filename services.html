BEFORE YOU TAKE A SEAT

A little clarity.
A better cut.

Not sure which service to choose? Tell us what you have in mind when you enquire.

Can I bring a reference photo?

Absolutely. A reference helps explain the shape and finish you like. Your barber can discuss how to adapt it to your hair.

How do I request an appointment?

Choose your service and visit the Contact page. Once the shop’s contact details are connected, you can send your request through WhatsApp. Appointments are confirmed by the shop.

Do I need to know exactly what I want?

No. Start with what you like or what you would like to change. The consultation helps shape the direction.
